About me

I am a Research Scientist at California Institute of Technology and NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory affiliate in California, USA, mainly interested in land-atmosphere interactions and their responses to global change, with emphasis on the global carbon cycle.

My research aims to better represent mechanistic processes in land-surface models with the goals of:

  1. Understanding fundamental controls of photosynthesis including transfer of radiation in vegetation canopies and nutrient uptake by plants.
  2. Using state-of-the-art process-based models to refine local and global representations of carbon, nitrogen, and phosphorus cycles.
  3. Developing more informed projections of plant growth, especially under future climate scenarios.
