Research

My work focuses on mechanistic representation of land surface processes in Earth system models, especially for photosynthesis, carbon uptake, nutrient constraints, and model-data integration.

Current themes

  • Canopy structure effects on radiative transfer, fluorescence, and photosynthesis.
  • Nitrogen and phosphorus acquisition costs in global carbon cycle modeling.
  • Mycorrhizal controls on carbon and nutrient cycling at global scale.
  • Benchmarking and diagnosis of next-generation land models.
  • Hyperspectral constraints for land surface and ecosystem process representation.
